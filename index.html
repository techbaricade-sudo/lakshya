About Lakshya Academy

Building bright futures with academic rigor and care

Lakshya Academy, established in Saharsa, Bihar, is a dedicated coaching institute for students of classes 6th to 9th. We provide specialized coaching in Mathematics, Science, English, Social Science, and Hindi through interactive classroom sessions, regular performance tracking, and doubt-clearing workshops. Our mission is to bridge the gap between school curriculum and competitive mindset.
